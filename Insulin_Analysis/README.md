# Structural and Coordination Environment Analysis of Zinc-Stabilized Insulin Hexamer

This repository focuses on the structural and metalloprotein analysis of the Insulin Hexamer utilizing structural bioinformatics tools.

## 🧬 Project Overview
* **PDB ID:** 1BEN (Insulin Hexamer)
* **Objective:** Investigating the metal-ligand coordination environment crucial for the structural stabilization and therapeutic formulation of insulin.

## 🧪 Methodology
* **Core Visualization:** Utilized **PyMOL** for the 3D macromolecular visualization of the insulin hexameric complex.
* **Coordination Analysis:** Identified and analyzed the coordinate bonds between the central Zinc metal ion (Zn²⁺) and the neighboring Histidine amino acid residues using PyMOL's Measurement Wizard.

## 💡 Key Insights & Structural Significance
* **Measured Bond Distance:** The coordination bond length was measured to be exactly **2.1 Å**.
* **Biological Relevance:** This 2.1 Å bond distance perfectly aligns with classic transition-metal coordination geometry in metalloproteins. This specific coordination network is critical for holding the hexameric unit together, maintaining insulin stability during storage, and regulating its active monomeric release into the bloodstream.
